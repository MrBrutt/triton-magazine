Заметки о Японии

В конце ноября 2025 года студенты гимназии побывали в незабываемой образовательной поездке по Японии с компанией-партнером "Олимпикс" и посетили около 10 городов.

Помимо основной программы в Осаке, Киото и Токио, которые впечатлили всех цветением красного клена, безопасной городской средой, гастрономическими удовольствиями и утонченной атмосферой традиционных храмов, студенты могли выбрать экскурсии по своему желанию: замок белой цапли в Химедзи; Кинодзаву - исторический город, сохранивший атмосферу эпохи Эдо; Хиросиму, где ребята посетили Мемориал Мира и исторический замок Карпа; город Нара - бывшую столицу Японии, которая славится своей умиротворенностью, размеренным ритмом жизни и парком с ручными оленями; Оцу - живописный курорт на берегу озера Бива; а часть нашей группы отправилась в дальнее путешествие к горе Фудзи, которая на удачу предстала перед ними в полной своей красе и оставила незабываемые впечатления! Студенты имели прекрасную возможность попрактиковать английский язык, прикоснуться к истории этой прекрасной страны и узнать много интересных особенностей японской культуры, кухни, транспортной системы и быта жителей страны восходящего солнца. Поистине незабываемые эмоции и множество ярких впечатлений остались у ребят в памяти после поездки, а гиды нашей группы подготовили прекрасный альбом с полной маршрутной картой, пояснениями и фотографиями, которые еще долго будут радовать всех нас!

We arrived in Osaka on the first day. After arriving, we walked around local markets and then explored the city in the evening. Osaka was very lively and full of lights and people.

On the first day, I lost my suitcase. The bus we traveled on did not bring my suitcase to the hotel. Because of that, I spent the whole trip without my personal things. At first, it was stressful, but later it became an unforgettable part of the journey.

We stayed in Osaka for a few more days. During this time, we went on excursions, walked around the city, and explored different areas. Osaka felt energetic and fun.

After that, we traveled to Kyoto. We stayed there for about two days or a little more. We went on excursions and walked around the city. Kyoto felt calmer and more traditional compared to Osaka. We also visited the Kyoto observation tower and saw the city from above.

Then we went to Kanazawa for about half a day. This name literally means "marsh of gold", it is said to derive from the legend of the peasant Imohori Togoro (“Potato-digger"), who was digging for potatoes when flakes of gold washed up. We walked around the city and enjoyed its quiet atmosphere. Kanazawa felt peaceful and authentic.

Next, we traveled to Tokyo. Tokyo was vibrant and modern. I did a lot of shopping, ate tasty food and walked around different districts. We also visited the famous Shibuya Crossing and had a great time in the city.

Later, we went to see Mount Fuji. Seeing Mount Fuji in real life was very impressive! The view was breathtaking, and it was one of the most memorable moments of the trip.

Overall, Japan is an amazing country. I really enjoyed the cities, the atmosphere, and the experience. Even with unexpected situations like losing my suitcase, the trip was unforgettable!

Our trip to Mount Fuji began early in the morning. The journey took several hours, but the anticipation of the upcoming views made the travel time fly by. Our guide shared interesting facts about the Japanese education system and student life, which added new dimensions to our understanding of the country.

When we arrived, the sky was clear, and Mount Fuji stood before us in all its glory. Its beauty was truly breathtaking — this moment was absolutely unforgettable. The impressions of the majestic mountain will stay with all of us for a lifetime.

One day we went to see the Golden Pavilion, a Zen Buddhist temple in Kyoto. It is a World Heritage Site and the two top floors of it are coated in 0,5 gold leaf. It was amazing! it was evening and the views were insane. We walked around the castle and the lake, took photos and couldn't get enough of the beauty. Hard to believe that I was there not so long ago. What a magnificent place! Majestic architecture and beautiful nature of Japan captivated me and took my heart!

One day in Hiroshima

At nine o’clock we arrived in historic Hiroshima by Shinkansen from Osaka. When we saw the panorama of the city from the station, which was the first city in the world to be nuclearly bombed, we were surprised at how beautiful and modern it was. At first, we went to Hiroshima Castle where we took a group photo and of course visited the museum. We learned a lot about the history of Hiroshima.

We reached the observation deck, we were amazed by the breathtaking and stunning view of the entire city!

After lunch, my friends and I went to the Peace Memorial Park, where we saw the only building that survived the nuclear bombing, the Atomic Dome. Also, in the same place we visited the Memorial Museum of Peace, which consists of two buildings - the Main and the Eastern. We managed to visit only the Main Hall, as we came late in the evening and it was closing soon. There was an exhibition related to the atomic bombing of Japan, which includes materials that allow visitors to experience the effects of the explosion, fire, radiation exposure and radiation diseases.

Our trip ended there. We boarded the Shinkansen again, arrived at the hotel in Osaka and fell asleep soundly. For me Hiroshima is both a memorial to the great tragedy of the 20th century that took away many lives, and a reminder to the whole humanity of how delicate peace and quiet are.

One day in Ginza

Ginza is the most famous and prestigious shopping district in Tokyo, Japan, it’s a center of high fashion, shopping and culture, known for its luxury boutiques, department stores, fine restaurants, art galleries, as well as one of the most expensive areas of the city.

Our hotel was located in Tokyo, so we got to Ginza by metro. It was such a lovely experience there, because we could see people of different nationalities, the way they talk to each other and what habits they have and also how the metro works and what a complex network it is. Honestly, we got a big list of things that we wanted to buy, so we headed for shops, but on the way we observed beautiful modern architecture, glass skyscrapers and fancy boutiques. Our guide let us go for a walk on our own, and at first, we couldn't decide where to go and got lost. But the Japanese people were very helpful and we managed to do some shopping. In the late afternoon, different lights came on and it was really worth it, and then we went back to the hotel.

Ginza was a great experience for us!

On our last full day, we went to see Fuji. The way there was not easy, it took us about 4.5 hours to get there by bus and we had to leave early in the morning to make it before dark. As soon as we arrived, we were astonished by the incredible views. We were so lucky! The weather was clear and Fuji revealed itself in all its beauty. This was the most memorable moment and a dream come true! I would love to go to Fuji again one day!
