Интервью с Дороти Недяк

В этом учебном году в Гимназию поступила студентка из Канады. Дороти приехала из Онтарио, где прошла среднюю школу онлайн в VLC (Virtual Learning Centre). Она рассказала, чем канадская школа отличается от российской, и в чем же заключаются достоинства и недостатки онлайн и очной школ.

- What differences can you dis-tinguish between this and your last school?

- Well, the fact that in person or on-line reflects on the difference between the structure of timetables here than comparing to my previous school. Here it is full-time school, and the class breaks are much longer, and classes are more spread out, while in my previous school the classes are scheduled only in the earlier part of the day.

Гимназия - очная школа, и перемены в классах намного длиннее, а занятия более рассредоточены, тогда как в моей предыдущей школе занятия распланированы только в первой половине дня.

Of course, there would be windows, but it is quite different. In the Ontario school system, there are 30 student courses over 4 high school years, and half of them are manda-tory, but there are very many courses that students can choose from and that gives a lot of flexibility in exploying in interests and direct at practical skills or exploring a cer-tain career path.

В школьной системе Онтарио старшая школа - это 4 года обучения и 30 предметов. Половина из них обязательны, но есть много предметов по выбору, и это позволяет варьировать свое расписание и развиваться в интересном тебе направлении.

- Does it mean you can choose from many courses the ones you like?

- Yes. Of course, there are the basic 8 courses and some of them are: English, math, science, history, but there are also other courses (business, introduction to technology, e.g.) student gets to choose. The biggest difference is that while here I must juggle ten different subjects, I would have to deal with four and that makes it much easier to focus, here I must spread out.

- I think it is more tangible for high school students. On average, how many lessons did you have in your previous school?

- Just like here it can depend on the day. One course has two 75-minute lessons per week, so for 1 course I am expected to put in 1,5 hours to 2 hours per day on aver-age, of course some subjects will be harder, some easier.

- It sounds like there is more flexi-bility of lessons in your previous school system than in ours. Here the schedule can be differed only when you choose our profile subjects in high school, could you change it in your middle school?

- I can’t talk about middle school because I only really got into proper, official school last year. Before that I was on the home school.

- Interesting, tell us more about your home school, is it better than the formal one?

- At home school what you do is explore whatever topics or curriculum you specifically prefer. The disadvantage is that there may be some areas that you have not covered properly, so, for example, I am very grateful that I have a good math foundation, but I have hardly done any physics, so it is very important to organize.

But it is also good that in home school you learn how to motivate yourself.

При дистанцонном формате самое важное - организовать свой рабочий процесс.

На домашнем обучении ты учишься мотивировать себя.

- I agree, it is always harder to motivate yourself without the outside “pusher”. A bit about the language: how did you learn Russian, was it hard to you?

- Since I was small, I had some ba-sic-basic Russian, just from everyday sce-nario because I have Russian family mem-bers, but once I needed to start learning Russian to come here, at first, I panicked. But weekly lessons and conversations helped me, I learned to absorb the flow of the language.

It is interesting, because the language structure is different from English, and I still have major issues with grammatical accura-cy, with endings…

- I feel like if I didn’t live always in Russia, I wouldn’t learn the language. Which was the first book you read in Russian?

- I decided to start with an easier book because I was still learning. I just watched “Гостья из будущего”, so I decid-ed to read the first book about Alisa. It is just a short book, but I found it extremely funny!

- I watched the film, and it was nice. So, from language we come to communication – is it difficult for you to communicate with Russian students?

- Sometimes I am more comfortable to say something in Russian because there are common phrases. I don’t have a prob-lem when I need to get point across, stu-dents here tend to know English. However, in conversations often I prefer not to follow, I may not understand in contexts.

- About Russia in general – do you maybe have some associations with Russia or some phrases that describe it? First time when you heard that you are coming here, what did you think?

- The phrase that pops to mind is that foreigners associate with the country is that Russia is an exotic place. But although I’ve been here for short time, I am learning to be more comfortable here. When I was first sightseeing around, I was particularly struck by the rich culture and history that has formed Russia how it is now.

- From the outside it sounds like an exotic and a various place.

- Exotic has an connotation of being distant, but I wouldn’t say so now – I am getting more fond of the place where I am now, so it is not true anymore.

- I, personally, have never studied abroad for a long time, so this is very interesting for me, and I hope it will be interesting for gymnasium’s students. Когда я в первый раз отправилась на осмотр достопримечательностей, я была поражена богатством культуры и истории, которые сделали Россию такой, какая она сейчас.
