title: Not forgetting
page: 38
toc: Not forgetting

{en} Once I met a man,
rough in those years.
But his words, once heard,
in my mind I still bear.

{en} Through a dark gallery,
I walked the empty hall,
Caught his soft voice,
a captivating call.

{en} "On the canvas, a girl in evening's grace,
A sunbird sang a gentle tune,
Paradise, fragments of tears on her face.
Morning, peace, dreams' delicate monsoon.

{en} The sun played in her flowing hair,
Yet in her eyes, not joy, but despair.
What happened to youth's tender glow?
Why does no one seem to know?

{en} The enchantress gazed up high,
Beyond the edge of the earthly sky.
Where she looked, ships sailed away,
Leaving behind their native bay.

{en} Sailing to a distant, unknown shore,
Abandoning the port forevermore.
Like a moth drawn to their light,
She forgot her home, lost in their sight."

{en} The old man shed a tear or two,
Raised his head, frozen in his rue.
Not from memory his silent lips
Whispered lightly in a soft eclipse:

{en} "Oh, great heaven, grant me peace,
let this eternal ache revoke.
For my sins, whatever they may be,
Quietly take this pain from me.

{en} Four years straight, no sleep embraced,
With no food I prayed and waited,
And you, cold almighty sky,
I cursed at night and couldn’t fly!"
.
